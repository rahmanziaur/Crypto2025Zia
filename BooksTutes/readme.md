# ICT 4115: Cryptography and Cyber Law

### B.Sc. (Engg.) in ICT | Fall Semester | MBSTU

Welcome to the course resource repository for **Cryptography and Cyber Law (ICT 4115)** offered to students of the Department of Information and Communication Technology (ICT), Mawlana Bhashani Science and Technology University (MBSTU), Tangail, Bangladesh.

This repository provides supplementary lecture materials, online courses, textbooks, lecture notes, and other learning resources to help students understand cryptographic principles, security mechanisms, and their applications in modern computing systems.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By following the course materials and recommended resources, students should be able to:

- Explain the fundamental principles and objectives of cryptography.
- Apply mathematical concepts used in cryptographic algorithms.
- Understand symmetric-key and public-key cryptography.
- Explain cryptographic hash functions, message authentication, and digital signatures.
- Understand key exchange, key management, and public-key infrastructure (PKI).
- Analyse cryptographic protocols and their security properties.
- Explore post-quantum cryptography and emerging cryptographic techniques.
- Understand the legal, ethical, and professional responsibilities associated with cybersecurity and cryptographic technologies.

**Resource:** [Cryptography 101: Lecture Notes](https://cryptography101.ca/lecture-notes/)

This collection includes lecture notes and supplementary materials on several important cryptographic topics.

Recommended materials include:

- [Public-Key Cryptography](https://cryptography101.ca/lecture-notes/) — introductory concepts, encryption, and digital signatures.
- [RSA and Elliptic-Curve Cryptography](https://cryptography101.ca/lecture-notes/) — mathematical foundations and public-key techniques.
- [Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://cryptography101.ca/lecture-notes/) — cryptographic techniques designed to address threats from quantum computers.
- [Lattice-Based Cryptography](https://cryptography101.ca/lattice-based-cryptography/) — lattice problems, Learning With Errors (LWE), Short Integer Solutions (SIS), and quantum-resistant cryptographic schemes.
